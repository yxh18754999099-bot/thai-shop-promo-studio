# Requirements: Promo video pipeline
1. WHEN a seller uploads a product photo and Thai text THEN the system SHALL produce a 9:16 MP4 under 30 s.
2. The video SHALL include a Thai female voiceover reading the text.
3. The video SHALL include Thai captions synced to the voiceover, in a bold pop-up style.
4. IF the GPU video model is unavailable THEN the system SHALL fall back to a Ken Burns animation of the photo.
